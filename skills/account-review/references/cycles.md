# Cycle check

The mechanical step. For each system under contract, determine what is due,
coming due within 90 days, and overdue.

## Read this first

The frequencies below are the common baseline and exist to make sure nothing gets
overlooked. They are a checklist, not an authority.

**The adopted edition and the local AHJ govern.** Jurisdictions adopt different
editions of NFPA 10, 25, and 72, and local amendments are routine. Before a
frequency goes in front of a customer, confirm it against the edition adopted in
that jurisdiction. Where you are working from this list rather than a verified
adopted edition, say so in the review.

Never state a frequency to a customer as settled when it has not been checked.

## Water-based systems (NFPA 25)

Prompts to check, not a complete schedule:

- Gauges, quarterly and annual items
- Control valves, frequency depends on whether they are locked or supervised
- Waterflow alarm device testing
- Main drain test
- Antifreeze solution testing
- **Five-year internal obstruction investigation.** The most commonly missed item
  in the entire business, and one of the largest single line items. Check the last
  performed date on every account, every review.
- Standpipe and hose system testing on its longer cycle
- Backflow forward flow test, plus whatever the water authority separately
  requires for backflow certification

## Fire pump (NFPA 25)

- No-flow (churn) test on its short cycle
- **Annual flow test.** Frequently missing entirely from the record on accounts
  that only bought sprinkler inspection. Check whether it has ever been done.
- Pump controller and transfer switch items

## Fire alarm (NFPA 72)

- Annual inspection and testing of initiating and notification devices
- Smoke detector sensitivity testing on its multi-year cycle
- Secondary power and battery testing
- Off-premises transmission and monitoring verification
- Panel age. Obsolete and end-of-life panels are both a compliance exposure and
  a real project. Note the model and manufacture year when known.

## Extinguishers (NFPA 10)

- Monthly quick check, usually the owner's responsibility, worth confirming they
  are actually doing it
- Annual maintenance
- **Six-year internal maintenance**
- **Hydrostatic test**, cycle length varies by extinguisher type

The six-year and hydro cycles are where extinguisher accounts quietly expand.
Pull the manufacture and last-service dates off the inventory and count forward.
A hundred-unit portfolio always has a batch coming due.

## Output of this step

A table: system, item, last performed, next due, status.

Status is Current, Due, or Overdue. Nothing else.

Flag separately anything with **no record at all**, which is different from
overdue and usually more interesting. No record often means the service was never
sold, not that it lapsed.
