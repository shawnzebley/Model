# Hooks

The first two lines are all LinkedIn shows before "see more." They decide whether
the post gets read.

Standard marketing hook libraries mostly violate `shawn-voice`. The bans below
are not preferences, they are that skill's rules applied to hooks.

## Banned

**"Unpopular opinion:"** and every contrarian-flag opener. Staged provocation.

**"Here's the thing"** / **"The real question is"** / **"And that's what matters."**
Fake epiphanies. Banned outright by `shawn-voice`.

**"It's not X, it's Y."** The false-contrast reframe. Say what it is.

**"I was wrong about..."** as a device when he was not actually wrong. Fine if
true, and only then.

**Anything with an em dash.** Comma or period.

**"In today's fast-paced world"** and every cousin of it.

**Question hooks that restate the topic.** "Ever wonder what happens during a
fire alarm inspection?" Start with the answer instead.

## What works

**The finding.** Lead with what was found, stated flatly.
- "Found a closed control valve on a quarterly last week."
- "Three heads out of spec in a building that passed last year."

**The stakes, understated.** The gap between the small finding and the large
consequence does the work. Do not close the gap for the reader in line one.
- "A $40 part was the only thing between that building and a dry riser."

**The question a customer asked.** Real, quoted, plain.
- "A property manager asked me why her sprinkler inspection is quarterly and her
  neighbor's is annual."

**The correction.** Something widely believed that is wrong, stated without
flourish.
- "A red tag is not a fine. It is a clock."

**The date and place.** Concrete openers are strong for this audience.
- "Saturday morning call on a backflow in Newark."

## Construction

Line one is the fact. Line two gives the reason to keep reading. Neither line
sells anything.

Then the body. Then stop when the point is made. `shawn-voice` bans a summarizing
closing paragraph, and that applies here too.

## Testing a hook

Read the first two lines alone. Would a property manager who has never heard of
Sobieski read line three?

If the hook only works because the reader already trusts Shawn, it is not a hook.
